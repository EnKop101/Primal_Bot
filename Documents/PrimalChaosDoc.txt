Primal Chaos is a major overhaul mod for Ark Survival Ascended.
It adds new creatures, tiers, bosses, and items.
It significantly increases the difficulty of the game.
The mod was made for PVE(Player Version Environment) and is not balanced nor do we recommend using it for PvP.


Many new creatures with unique tiers and abilities.
Spawn codes can be found in a linked spreadsheet. (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1cT7InAARC7WxlWVByin3DLlLE5HZ1e6P93UNyO2Kl1A/edit?usp=sharing)

Some creatures, like some of the bosses, require defeat before dropping a tameable token.

Each element (Volcanic, Hydro, Electric) has strengths and weaknesses.
Use the correct element and potions for effective boss fights.
Modded dinos are generally stronger than vanilla dinos and bosses.

The current final boss for Primal Chaos is the Deathknight, a very powerful opponent.
The Deathknight has no elemental weaknesses and various strategies can be used to defeat him.Do what works best for you.
Defeating the Deathknight has a 10% chance to spawn a tameable token.
The Deathknight can only be summoned.

All the dino tiers include (This is in order from weakest to strongest.):
Beta, Alpha, Elementals, Advanced Elementals Mythic, Legendary, Primal Resurrected, Demonic, Angelic and Nightmares
The strongest dino tier is the Demonic and Angelic while the weakest are Beta tier.

There is a 3 kinds of variants for the Elemental dino tier
Volcanic, Hydro and Electric.
There is also a Guardian boss for each elemental variant.
The Elementals each have their own strength and weaknesses which needs to be considered before fighting one of them.
The weaknesses and strength goes like this. Volcanic > Hydro > Electric > Volcanic.

For the Advanced elementals there are 2 variants.
Fairies and Shadow

Egg laying speed can be increased through server configs or by taming an Oviraptor to get the egg laying boost.
