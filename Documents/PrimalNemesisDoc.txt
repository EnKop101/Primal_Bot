Primal Nemesis is a premium overhaul mod for Ark Survival Ascended.
It adds new creatures, tiers, bosses, and items.
It significantly increases the difficulty of the game.

Final Boss:
The final boss is Nemesis, a powerful creature that can only be summoned.

Cost:
The mod costs around $10 on CurseForge.

Taming Bionic Tier Dinos:
Bionic tier dinos can be tamed by knocking them out and feeding them Bionic kibble.
